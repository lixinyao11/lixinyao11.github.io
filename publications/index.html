  1. ContactMimic: Humanoid Object Interaction via Contact Control
    Xinyao Li, Xialin He, Runpei Dong, and Saurabh Gupta
    CoRL 2026
    Xinyao Li and Xialin He contributed equally. University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML Code

    Keypoint tracking alone is insufficient for object interaction tasks such as sitting on a chair, wiping a board, or pushing furniture, where the robot can reach the correct pose without making meaningful physical contact with the object. We present ContactMimic, a learning framework that tracks explicit part-level binary contact commands alongside keypoint trajectories. ContactMimic is made possible through the use of contact-following rewards and a trajectory augmentation scheme aimed at breaking the correlations between keypoint trajectories and contact labels. The resulting policy successfully decouples contact behavior from keypoint geometry, and achieves precise physical contact as well as contact-controllability (produce or suppress contact during deployment as desired). Simulation experiments across 10 diverse human-object interaction motions confirm that ContactMimic exhibits contact controllability that enables it to complete manipulation tasks without task-specific rewards, while also outperforming keypoint-only trackers on contact-relevant tasks. Ablations confirm the necessity of the proposed trajectory augmentation scheme and sim2real deployment validates contact controllability in the real world across 5 different motions.

  2. ULTRA: Unified Multimodal Control for Autonomous Humanoid Whole-Body Loco-Manipulation
    Xialin He, Sirui Xu, Xinyao Li, Runpei Dong, Liuyu Bian, Yu-Xiong Wang, and Liang-Yan Gui
    IROS 2026
    Xialin He and Sirui Xu contributed equally.
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML

    ULTRA is an all-in-one controller for humanoid loco-manipulation: track when references exist; act from egocentric perception and sparse intent when they don’t.
